## 2. Information We Collect & How We Use It (डेटा संग्रह एवं उपयोग)

### A. Google Sign-In & User Profile
- When you choose to **Sign In with Google**, we receive your basic public profile information provided by Google OAuth:
  - **Full Name / Display Name**
  - **Email Address**
  - **Profile Picture URL (if available)**
- **Purpose:** This information is strictly used to create and identify your personalized Sadhak profile and to securely save and sync your lifetime chanting statistics across devices and app reinstalls.
- **Privacy Guarantee:** We **NEVER** sell, rent, monetize, or share your email address or personal details with any third-party marketing or advertising networks.

### B. Sadhana Statistics & Chanting History
- When logged in, your chanting data (bead counts, completed malas, and daily timestamped history) is securely associated with your account.
- In Guest Mode (when not logged in), chanting counts exist only in temporary memory and are not stored in any external database.

### C. Device & Operational Information
- Non-sensitive technical identifiers required for app functionality (e.g., screen dimensions, OS version, language preference) are processed locally on your device.

## 3. Permissions & Device Features (ऐप अनुमतियाँ)

### A. Microphone Permission (वॉइस जाप पहचान)
- **Use Case:** To allow voice-activated chanting detection (बोलकर जाप करना).
- **Processing:** The microphone input is processed **strictly in real-time on your device** to detect mantra phrases (e.g., "ॐ नमः शिवाय", "जय सियाराम", "हरे कृष्ण").
- **Zero Audio Storage:** We **DO NOT record, store, transmit, or upload your voice audio** to any external server. Once the spoken mantra is recognized, the audio data is instantly discarded.

### B. Vibration & Keep-Awake Permission
- **Haptic Vibration:** Used to provide gentle tactile feedback on bead completion and 108-bead mala milestones.
- **Screen Keep-Awake:** Used during active auto-chanting or voice chanting sessions so your meditation is uninterrupted by phone screen timeouts.
